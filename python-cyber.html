Global Cybersecurity Threat Analysis

Exploring attack losses, threat patterns, and response times across cybersecurity incidents.

Illustrative comparison of sample averages reported in the notebook; not a population estimate.

The business question

Which cyberattack patterns appear most costly, and how do response times vary across defense mechanisms? I explored whether incident data could help frame operational and financial risk questions.

My role & process

I completed an individual Python project using two related datasets covering 100 cybersecurity incident records from 2023–24. I merged the incident and detail files, inspected the resulting data, explored distributions, and performed statistical tests.

Key findings

In this sample, SQL injection incidents had average reported losses above $56 million, followed by man-in-the-middle incidents above $53 million. AI-based detection showed an average resolution time of 35.4 hours, more than six hours faster than firewall-dependent organizations in the sample.

However, the ANOVA examining defense mechanism and resolution time produced a p-value of approximately 0.84, so this apparent difference was not statistically significant in the analyzed sample.

Challenges & limitations

Data preparation required merging related files and checking field consistency. With just 100 records, findings are sensitive to sample composition. The observed associations should not be interpreted as causal relationships or representative industry benchmarks.

Business value

The analysis demonstrates a responsible approach to risk analytics: identify meaningful questions, examine evidence, report uncertainty, and avoid overclaiming results. The notebook provides a reproducible record of the work.
