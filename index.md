## 🌍 Supported Connection Types

ZedSecure's strength is variety. Here is what each family of connection is good for, described without technical jargon:

- **Xray family (VLESS, VMess, XTLS)** — modern, fast connections that work well on mobile networks and are good at getting through restrictions.
- **sing-box** — a flexible engine that supports many connection styles in one place.
- **Shadowsocks and SOCKS5** — lightweight proxy methods, excellent for speed on stable networks.
- **Psiphon** — a circumvention tool designed for networks that block access to websites and apps.
- **Tor** — routes your traffic through several relays so your origin is very hard to trace. Slower, but very private.
- **DNS tunnels** — useful on locked-down networks where ordinary connections are blocked but basic name lookups still work.
- **WireGuard and AmneziaWG** — modern, efficient VPN tunnels known for speed and low battery use.
- **OpenConnect** — connects to many corporate and university VPN gateways.
- **IKEv2** — a widely supported standard that reconnects quickly when you switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data.

You do not need to understand all of these. Most users settle on one or two favourites and ignore the rest.

## 🧭 Troubleshooting

**The app will not connect.**
Try a different connection from your list. Some networks block certain methods and allow others.

**Pages load slowly.**
Switch to a lighter connection type, or move closer to your router. Anonymity modes are intentionally slower.

**The connection drops when Wi-Fi changes.**
Turn on automatic reconnection in settings.

**Nothing happens when I press connect.**
Close the app completely and open it again. If the problem continues, check the log view for the last message and share it when asking for help.

**Windows asks for permission when I open the app.**
This is a normal security prompt for downloaded applications. Confirm it to continue.
